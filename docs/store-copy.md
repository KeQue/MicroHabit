### Description

Microhabit helps you stay consistent with small daily habits through shared accountability.

Create or join a league, commit to one activity for the month, and check in each day as you build momentum with your group.

Features:

- Create or join habit leagues
- Track daily monthly check-ins
- Switch between personal progress and ranking views
- Get gentle reminder notifications
- Stay motivated with streaks, rankings, and progress tracking

Microhabit is designed for simple, social consistency. Small habits, real accountability.

### Full Description

Microhabit helps you stay consistent through shared accountability.

Create or join a league, commit to a daily activity, and check in across the month. Follow your own progress in My View or compare your standing in Ranking.

Use Microhabit to:

- Track daily habit completion
- Build streaks and monthly momentum

Microhabit focuses on simple structure, consistency, and social motivation.
