# Microhabit Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

This draft is a starting point for Microhabit's public privacy policy. Replace bracketed placeholders before publishing.

## Who We Are

Microhabit is a habit and accountability app that lets users create or join leagues, track daily check-ins, and receive reminders.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: [support email]

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Account information such as email address and password credentials handled through our authentication provider
- Profile information such as name, username, and plan tier
- League data such as league name, activity, membership, and check-in history
- Device and app information needed for crash reporting, analytics, and notifications
- Notification tokens if you enable reminders

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Let you create, join, and participate in leagues
- Track check-ins and rankings
- Send reminders and support app functionality
- Monitor crashes, diagnose issues, and improve the app
- Measure basic product usage and funnel health

## Analytics and Monitoring

Microhabit may collect limited analytics events such as app opens, sign-ins, sign-ups, league creation, league joining, first check-in, and reminder-related events.

If crash reporting is enabled, crash reports may include technical device and app information needed to diagnose issues.

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Microhabit may send reminders to your device. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as:

- Supabase for authentication and database services
- [Crash reporting provider]
- [Analytics provider, if any beyond internal analytics]

## Data Retention

We retain account and league data for as long as needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, unless you delete your account.

## Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account from within the app. Account deletion permanently removes your account and related data, subject to any limited retention required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

If the in-app deletion flow is unavailable, contact: [support email]

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Microhabit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## International Users

If you use Microhabit from outside the country where it is operated, your information may be processed in other countries where our service providers operate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above when changes are made.

## Contact
