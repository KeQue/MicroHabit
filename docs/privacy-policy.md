# Commito Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Commito helps people create or join monthly accountability leagues with friends.
This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is used, and how
users can request deletion.

## Information We Collect

Commito may collect and store:

- Account information: email address, user id, display name, and username.
- League information: league names, activity names, invite codes, membership,
  roles, and league progress.
- Habit activity: daily completion logs for the leagues a user joins.
- Authentication information: sign-in provider details handled through Supabase
  Auth.
- Basic technical information that may be processed by services used to run the
  app, such as Supabase and Expo.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Let users create, join, and participate in leagues.
- Show league members, progress, rankings, and activity history.
- Support invite-code sharing.
- Understand product use through aggregate counts of saved league creation,
  participation, check-ins, and activity across weeks and months.
- Keep the app secure and reliable.
- Provide support when users contact us.

We do not sell personal information.

These participation reports use the account and league records already stored
to run Commito. They do not add an advertising or analytics SDK. Report output
contains counts rather than names, email addresses, or invitation codes. The
underlying records remain personal information and follow this policy's access
and deletion rules.

## League Visibility

League members can see information needed for the league experience, including
member display names, activity progress, and rankings. Invite codes allow other
users with the code to open or join the related league.

## Free Beta And Payments

The current beta offers free leagues. Paid league purchases, reward payouts,
and charity payments are not enabled in this beta.

Commito does not store card numbers or bank account details.

## Notifications

Commito may ask permission to send reminders. Users can allow or deny
notifications in the app or device settings. Denying notifications does not
block core app functionality.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. App versions with reminder
controls let you choose a local time or disable reminders in Settings. Earlier
versions use 17:00 device-local time. The reminder preference is stored on this
device and shared across accounts using this installation; it is not uploaded
to Commito. Reminders stop when signed out. Notification permissions and
scheduled reminders are managed on the device. Device settings can delay or
silence delivery.

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account inside the app from Settings. Account
deletion removes the user account, profile, league memberships, habit logs, and
related user data from the app database where technically possible.

Deleting an account also deletes leagues created by that account, including
the shared check-in history for all members of those leagues. This cannot be
undone.

Some records may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention,
financial, tax, or compliance reasons.

## Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, operate leagues,
meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When an
account is deleted, associated app data is deleted or anonymized where possible.

## Third-Party Services

Commito may use third-party services to operate the app, including:

- Supabase for authentication and database services.
- Expo for app development and delivery services.
- GitHub Pages for public support, privacy and invitation pages. An invitation
  page address contains its invite code; hosting providers may process page
  requests. The invitation page does not look up accounts or league activity.
- Apple and Google for supported sign-in methods and app distribution.
- Google's email service for account confirmation and password-reset emails
  sent through Supabase Auth.

These services may process information according to their own policies.

## Children's Privacy

Commito is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13
has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted
at the public privacy policy URL used in App Store Connect.
