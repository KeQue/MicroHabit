Commito Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Commito helps people create or join monthly accountability leagues with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Information We Collect

Commito may collect and store:

  • Account information: email address, user id, display name, and username.
  • League information: league names, activity names, invite codes, membership, roles, and league progress.
  • Habit activity: daily completion logs for the leagues a user joins.
  • Authentication information: sign-in provider details handled through Supabase Auth.
  • Notification preferences or device notification tokens if push notifications are enabled in a future version.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed by services used to run the app, such as Supabase and Expo.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Let users create, join, and participate in leagues.
  • Show league members, progress, rankings, and activity history.
  • Support invite-code sharing.
  • Keep the app secure and reliable.
  • Provide support when users contact us.

We do not sell personal information.

League Visibility

League members can see information needed for the league experience, including member display names, activity progress, and rankings. Invite codes allow other users with the code to open or join the related league.

Payments, Rewards, And Charity

If paid leagues are enabled, purchases are handled through Apple or Google in-app purchases and may be managed through RevenueCat. Commito may store purchase status, product type, league access, estimated reward information, and charity-related ledger information needed to operate the league.

Commito does not store card numbers or bank account details.

Notifications

Commito may ask permission to send reminders. Users can allow or deny notifications in the app or device settings. Denying notifications does not block core app functionality.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their account inside the app from the Account menu. Account deletion removes the user account, profile, league memberships, habit logs, and related user data from the app database where technically possible.

Some records may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, financial, tax, or compliance reasons.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, operate leagues, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When an account is deleted, associated app data is deleted or anonymized where possible.

Third-Party Services

Commito may use third-party services to operate the app, including:

  • Supabase for authentication and database services.
  • Expo for app development and delivery services.
  • Apple and Google for app distribution and in-app purchases.
  • RevenueCat for purchase infrastructure if paid leagues are enabled.

These services may process information according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Commito is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at the public privacy policy URL used in App Store Connect.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact commito.support@gmail.com.
